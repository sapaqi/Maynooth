About Maynooth DZ

Bringing people, places and practical climate action together — for a greener Maynooth.

The Decarbonising Zone

A greener Maynooth,
together.

A Decarbonising Zone is a place to bring local climate action into everyday life. It connects the things we can do in our homes, on our streets and across our community.

For Maynooth, that means exploring practical ways to use less energy, make more sustainable journeys and care for the green spaces around us.

There is no single action that does it all. The opportunity is to learn together, build on local ideas and make it easier for more people to take part.

Your questions,
answered.

New to the Zone? Start here.

What is a Decarbonising Zone?

A place where local climate action comes together — from improving homes and travel to supporting nature and community projects. The aim is to reduce emissions while making everyday life better.

Who can get involved?

Residents, community groups, schools and local businesses can all explore ways to take part. Visit the Community page or contact the Climate Action Office to start a conversation.

Where can I find local projects?

Browse the project directory by category or explore the interactive map. Each project page brings together its story and supporting information. Some projects do not display a location for privacy reasons.

What can I do at home?

The Take Action page is a starting point for exploring home retrofit. You can also find useful guides and further reading in Resources.

How do I share an idea or ask a question?

Contact the Climate Action Office at climateaction@kildarecoco.ie. Tell the team a little about your idea and how it relates to Maynooth.
